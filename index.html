The philosophy behind the name

Inversion. To protect assets under management, Charlie starts with what destroys them. Most platforms optimize for returns. Charlie optimizes for the decision a client makes at the worst possible moment, and works backward from there.

Avoiding stupidity over seeking brilliance. Charlie runs on deterministic math and historical precedent, not language-model speculation. The engine does not forecast markets. It shows clients what markets have actually done before, under comparable conditions.

Uninterrupted compounding. Every unnecessary liquidation at a market bottom is a permanent tax on long-term return. Charlie's job is narrow: prevent the redemptions that don't need to happen.

Named for Charlie Munger, who argued that long-term wealth accumulation is 10% intelligence and 90% temperament.

What Charlie does

Advisor Co-Pilot

Serving a $200K account takes your advisors the same three hours of reporting as a $5M account, which makes roughly 80% of your book unprofitable to service properly.

Charlie generates automated tax-drag audits and branded portfolio summaries in seconds, ready for an advisor's sign-off.

Compliance stance: rule-based administrative software running on advisor-configured parameters.

Market Signal Tracker

Insider filings are noisy. Routine stock grants and rebalancing trades bury the signal your analysts are actually looking for.

Charlie ingests and filters SEC Form 4, HKEX DI, and SGX disclosures to surface open-market C-suite purchases.

Compliance stance: factual regulatory data aggregation. No price targets or recommendations generated.

Anti-Panic Visualizer

When markets drop 10 to 20%, your clients don't need more news. They need to see what happens if they hold.

Charlie runs historical recovery matching and cost-of-panic calculations against the client's actual portfolio allocation, not a generic index.

Compliance stance: non-advisory historical data visualization. No buy or sell recommendations generated.

All Charlie outputs are draft materials for licensed human advisors to review, adjust, and approve. Charlie holds zero advisory discretion.
